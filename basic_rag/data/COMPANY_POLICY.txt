COMPANY HR POLICY

1. Paid Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 18 paid leaves per calendar year.

Employees can carry forward up to 5 unused paid leaves to the next calendar year.

Paid leave should normally be requested at least 3 working days before the intended leave date.

Employees who have completed less than 6 months of service are entitled to a prorated number of paid leaves based on their joining date.

2. Sick Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive working days may require a medical certificate.

Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible when taking unexpected sick leave.

3. Emergency Leave Policy

Emergency leave may be requested on the same day when an unexpected personal or family emergency occurs.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as possible.

Emergency leave requires manager approval.

4. Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Employees must remain available during their normal working hours while working from home.

Work-from-home requests should normally be submitted at least one working day in advance.

Employees working from home are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection and completing their assigned work.

5. Working Hours

The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday.

Normal working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees are expected to take a one-hour lunch break during the working day.

Employees who need to work outside normal working hours should obtain approval from their manager when required.

6. Remote Work Eligibility

Employees who have completed at least 3 months of service may request regular work-from-home arrangements.

New employees may be allowed to work from home occasionally at the discretion of their manager.

Remote work eligibility may depend on the employee's role and operational requirements.

7. Leave Approval

Planned leave is subject to manager approval.

Approval depends on team requirements, workload, and the availability of other team members.

Employees should not assume that a leave request has been approved until they receive confirmation from their manager.

8. Public Holidays

The company observes the public holidays listed in the annual company holiday calendar.

The holiday calendar may vary by employee location.

Employees should refer to the latest official holiday calendar for their location.

9. Leave During Probation

Employees may request leave during their probation period.

Leave approval during probation is subject to business requirements and manager approval.

Paid leave entitlement during probation is calculated according to the employee's applicable prorated entitlement.

10. Policy Updates

Company policies may be updated when business, legal, or operational requirements change.

Employees should always refer to the latest official version of the relevant company policy.

The latest approved policy document takes precedence over older versions.
